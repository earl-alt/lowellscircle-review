What’s the Circle?

Get in the Circle.

Is this just another one of Lowell’s crazy ideas? Maybe. Come find out.

Who it’s for

People who like to draw, and dance and write and take photos and speak. And the people who support those that do.

You don’t need a title, a portfolio or permission. If you make things, or want to, you’re who we’re looking for.

Where it comes from

The Circle grew out of Circle Creative Fest, held September 19, 2026, at Lawrence & Broadway in Uptown, Chicago. It was the first of what we want to be an annual celebration of the uniquely human ability to create.

Who’s Lowell

Lowell Thompson is a veteran Chicago adman and a pioneer of the city’s advertising scene. He is an executive producer of Channels Changers, the documentary about the Black admen and adwomen who cracked Chicago advertising from the late ’60s through the mid ’80s.
